About the hackathon

KLE Inter College Hackathon 2K26 is conducted by the Department of BCA and is open exclusively to students of KLE BCA College, Mahalingpur. Build a team, choose a domain, and ship a working prototype in front of the judges.

Our vision: turn classroom skills into products that solve real problems in healthcare, farming, finance, learning and beyond.
